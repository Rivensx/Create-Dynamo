## Create: Dynamo

Create: Dynamo is a realistic electrical and mechanical engineering add-on for Create. Go beyond basic rotational force by building complete power grids, refining fuels, running combustion engines, and generating real-world electricity measured in Volts (V), Amperes (A), and Watts (W).

⚡ Key Features

• Realistic Electrical Physics: Generate, store, and distribute electricity using real-world electrical units (V, A, W, and Wh).
• Dynamos & Alternators: Convert mechanical rotation (RPM and torque) into DC power with Dynamos, or generate high-output AC power using industrial Alternators.
• Combustion Engines & Fuels: Power your factories independently with Gasoline and Diesel Engines. Refine fossil fuels or produce sustainable Biofuels naturally from crops and organic materials.
• Renewable Wind Power: Construct large-scale wind turbines using custom Windmill Blades to harness clean, continuous energy.
• Energy Storage (Batteries): Store excess electricity in Batteries to keep your base running during peak loads or windless nights.
• Industrial Lighting & Appliances: Illuminate your factories and builds with realistic electric Lights powered directly by your grid.
• Vanilla+ Create Aesthetic: Fully animated engine pistons, spinning blades, copper coils, and analog gauges designed to match the classic Create look and feel.

📦 Requirements

• Minecraft [1.20.1 / 1.21.1]
• [Forge / NeoForge / Fabric]
